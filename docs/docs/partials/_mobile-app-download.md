The mobile app can be downloaded from the following places:

- Obtainium: You can get your Obtainium config link from the [Utilities page of your PizCloud server](https://photos.pizcloud.com/utilities).
- [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pizcloud.photos)
- [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pizcloud/id_)
- [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.pizcloud.photos)
